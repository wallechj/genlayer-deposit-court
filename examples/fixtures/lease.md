# Acme Rentals — Lease Agreement (excerpt)

Property: 12 Oak Street, Apt 4. Tenancy: 2 years. Deposit: 1 GEN.

1. The tenant is liable for damage beyond normal wear and tear. Damage caused during the tenancy, including broken windows and broken fixtures, is deducted from the deposit at repair cost.
2. Normal wear and tear is the landlord's cost: minor scuff marks, fading paint, small nail holes, and light carpet wear after a tenancy of one year or more are not deductible.
3. Utilities (electricity and water) are the tenant's responsibility. Any unpaid utility bill at move-out is deducted from the deposit at the billed amount.
4. The landlord must provide photo or inspection evidence for every deduction claimed.
5. Pre-existing damage noted on the move-in checklist is never deductible.
